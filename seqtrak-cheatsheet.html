SEQTRAKcheatsheet

Button combos for OS V2.00. “Hold” means keep it down while you press or turn the next control.

Most combos start by holding one of these

  • ALLmetronome, quantize, keyboard mode, mixer, mute mode, song mode, save sound, chord edit, count-in
  • PROJECTswitch / snapshot / delete project, launch quantize
  • PAGEstep input, paste, page & length
  • DELETEdelete pattern, p-lock, scene, sample pattern, reset swing
  • Drum keymicro timing, substep, probability, p-lock, note length

Source: Yamaha SEQTRAK User Guide, OS V2.00 edition (manual.yamaha.com). “REC” is the Record key; “SOUND PAGE” and “FX PAGE” are the page buttons next to the Sound Design knobs and touch sliders.

Holding a lit Drum key ≥2 s opens Substep on Drum tracks; in Step Input mode the same gesture copies steps (paste: hold PAGE + press Drum key).
